NaPiTA scoring update

- Classic and Speed Run only; Chaos mode has been removed from room creation.
- Each round has four categories and a maximum of 40 points.
- Accepted answer unique within its category: 10 points.
- Same accepted answer submitted by multiple players in the same category: 5 points for each player who used it.
- Blank or rejected answer: 0 points.
- Matching is case-insensitive and ignores leading/trailing/repeated whitespace.
- Unverified answers marked for review do not earn points until accepted by the existing appeal flow.
